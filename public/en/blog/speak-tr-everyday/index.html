About Karshvar

What is Karshvar?

I build and develop Karshvar in my spare time to make discovering the world easier and more enjoyable for people who, like me, love reading about human history and prehistory, and the natural history of Earth. My biggest difficulty was always keeping track of places on the map: from Anatolia to the Caucasus, from the Iranian plateau to the Americas, I would lose track of where important events happened and get confused by the different names used in each period.

So I had to learn geography, and this game/encyclopedia grew out of all that learning. It is both a way to test myself and a step toward a fun offline companion for exploring the world.

My ultimate aim is to build a visual tool for exploring the history of the world’s peoples, Earth’s natural history, and human prehistory. A tool that helps me see what the world was like, even before self-awareness existed on Earth! For me, discovering the past is more enjoyable than predicting the future.

Why the name Karshvar?

I am an Iranian from Khorasan, with a deep attachment to Iranian/Ir culture, which I feel part of. The name Karshvar comes from an ancient Iranian word related to the modern Persian word for “country.” In Avestan cosmology, the world had seven regions. If you would like to learn more, you can read about it on Wikipedia using the link below.

How to speak Turkish: everyday conversations for English speakers

Use these simple Turkish phrases to greet people, shop, order food, ask directions and check into a hotel. Every phrase includes its English meaning.

EnglishفارسیالعربيةFrançaisРусский
1

Greetings and politeness

Start with a greeting and add please to requests. Use excuse me to get someone's attention politely.

  • Merhaba.Hello.
    Pronunciation mer-ha-ba
  • Görüşürüz.Goodbye.
    Pronunciation gö-rü-shü-rüz
  • Lütfen.Please.
    Pronunciation lüt-fen
  • Teşekkür ederim.Thank you.
    Pronunciation te-shek-kür e-de-rim
  • Affedersiniz.Excuse me.
    Pronunciation af-fe-der-si-niz
  • Evet.Yes.
    Pronunciation e-vet
  • Hayır.No.
    Pronunciation ha-yuhr

Short conversation

  1. YouMerhaba.Hello.
  2. Other speakerMerhaba.Hello.
2

When you do not understand

If you miss an answer, ask the speaker to slow down or repeat it before continuing.

  • Anlamıyorum.I don't understand.
    Pronunciation an-la-muh-yo-rum
  • Lütfen yavaş konuşun.Please speak slowly.
    Pronunciation lüt-fen ya-vash ko-nu-shun
  • Lütfen tekrar eder misiniz?Could you repeat that, please?
    Pronunciation lüt-fen tek-rar e-der mi-si-niz

Short conversation

  1. YouLütfen yavaş konuşun.Please speak slowly.
  2. Other speakerEvet.Yes.
  3. YouTeşekkür ederim.Thank you.
3

Shopping and payment

Point to the item, ask the price and check whether you can pay by card. Acceptance depends on the shop.

  • Bu ne kadar?How much is this?
    Pronunciation bu ne ka-dar
  • Kartla ödeyebilir miyim?Can I pay by card?
    Pronunciation kart-la ö-de-ye-bi-lir mi-yim
  • Bunu istiyorum, lütfen.I would like this, please.
    Pronunciation bu-nu is-ti-yo-rum lüt-fen
  • Hayır, teşekkür ederim.No, thank you.
    Pronunciation ha-yuhr te-shek-kür e-de-rim

Short conversation

  1. YouKartla ödeyebilir miyim?Can I pay by card?
  2. Other speakerEvet.Yes.
  3. YouBunu istiyorum, lütfen.I would like this, please.
4

Cafés and restaurants

Ask for a table, place your order and request the bill at the end. Without meat does not communicate every dietary restriction.

  • İki kişilik bir masa, lütfen.A table for two, please.
    Pronunciation i-ki ki-shi-lik bir ma-sa lüt-fen
  • Su, lütfen.Water, please.
    Pronunciation su lüt-fen
  • Etsiz olsun, lütfen.Without meat, please.
    Pronunciation et-siz ol-sun lüt-fen
  • Hesap, lütfen.The bill, please.
    Pronunciation he-sap lüt-fen

Short conversation

  1. Youİki kişilik bir masa, lütfen.A table for two, please.
  2. Other speakerEvet.Yes.
  3. YouTeşekkür ederim.Thank you.
5

Asking directions

Use the station question to ask your way; the French and Russian phrases specifically refer to a railway station. Practise right and left as possible answers.

  • İstasyon nerede?Where is the station?
    Pronunciation is-tas-yon ne-re-de
  • Uzak mı?Is it far?
    Pronunciation u-zak muh
  • Sağa.To the right.
    Pronunciation saa
  • Sola.To the left.
    Pronunciation so-la

Short conversation

  1. Youİstasyon nerede?Where is the station?
  2. Other speakerSağa.To the right.
  3. YouTeşekkür ederim.Thank you.
6

Hotels and asking for help

At reception, mention your reservation and ask about check-out and Wi-Fi. Show the final phrase if you need help.

  • Rezervasyonum var.I have a reservation.
    Pronunciation re-zer-vas-yo-num var
  • Çıkış saati kaç?What time is check-out?
    Pronunciation chuh-kuhsh sa-a-ti kach
  • Wi-Fi şifresi nedir?What is the Wi-Fi password?
    Pronunciation vay-fay shif-re-si ne-dir
  • Bana yardım eder misiniz?Can you help me?
    Pronunciation ba-na yar-duhm e-der mi-si-niz

Short conversation

  1. YouBana yardım eder misiniz?Can you help me?
  2. Other speakerEvet.Yes.
  3. YouWi-Fi şifresi nedir?What is the Wi-Fi password?

How to practise

Choose one situation each day, read the phrases aloud and swap roles. Latin pronunciation guides are approximate and cannot represent every sound or stress precisely; show the original text when needed.
