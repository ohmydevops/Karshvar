About Karshvar

I built Karshvar to make discovering the world easier for people who love reading history. My biggest difficulty was always geography: from Anatolia to the Caucasus, from the Iranian plateau to Byzantium, I kept losing track of where important events happened.

So I started studying geography, and this game grew out of that learning. It is both a way to test myself and a step toward the offline companion I wanted for exploring the world. I plan to add more historical features over time.

Karshvar takes its name from an ancient Iranian word related to today’s Persian word for country. In Avestan cosmology, the world was described as seven regions.

How to speak French: everyday conversations for English speakers

Use these simple French phrases to greet people, shop, order food, ask directions and check into a hotel. Every phrase includes its English meaning.

1

Greetings and politeness

Start with a greeting and add please to requests. Use excuse me to get someone's attention politely.

  • Bonjour.Hello.
    Pronunciation bon-zhoor
  • Au revoir.Goodbye.
    Pronunciation oh ruh-vwar
  • S’il vous plaît.Please.
    Pronunciation seel voo pleh
  • Merci.Thank you.
    Pronunciation mehr-see
  • Excusez-moi.Excuse me.
    Pronunciation eks-kü-zay mwa
  • Oui.Yes.
    Pronunciation wee
  • Non.No.
    Pronunciation non

Short conversation

  1. YouBonjour.Hello.
  2. Other speakerBonjour.Hello.
2

When you do not understand

If you miss an answer, ask the speaker to slow down or repeat it before continuing.

  • Je ne comprends pas.I don't understand.
    Pronunciation zhuh nuh kom-pron pah
  • Parlez lentement, s’il vous plaît.Please speak slowly.
    Pronunciation par-lay lont-mon seel voo pleh
  • Pouvez-vous répéter, s’il vous plaît ?Could you repeat that, please?
    Pronunciation poo-vay voo ray-pay-tay seel voo pleh

Short conversation

  1. YouParlez lentement, s’il vous plaît.Please speak slowly.
  2. Other speakerOui.Yes.
  3. YouMerci.Thank you.
3

Shopping and payment

Point to the item, ask the price and check whether you can pay by card. Acceptance depends on the shop.

  • Combien ça coûte ?How much is this?
    Pronunciation kom-byen sah koot
  • Est-ce que je peux payer par carte ?Can I pay by card?
    Pronunciation es kuh zhuh puh pay-yay par kart
  • Je voudrais ceci, s’il vous plaît.I would like this, please.
    Pronunciation zhuh voo-dreh suh-see seel voo pleh
  • Non, merci.No, thank you.
    Pronunciation non mehr-see

Short conversation

  1. YouEst-ce que je peux payer par carte ?Can I pay by card?
  2. Other speakerOui.Yes.
  3. YouJe voudrais ceci, s’il vous plaît.I would like this, please.
4

Cafés and restaurants

Ask for a table, place your order and request the bill at the end. Without meat does not communicate every dietary restriction.

  • Une table pour deux, s’il vous plaît.A table for two, please.
    Pronunciation ün tabl poor duh seel voo pleh
  • De l’eau, s’il vous plaît.Water, please.
    Pronunciation duh lo seel voo pleh
  • Sans viande, s’il vous plaît.Without meat, please.
    Pronunciation son vyand seel voo pleh
  • L’addition, s’il vous plaît.The bill, please.
    Pronunciation lah-dee-syon seel voo pleh

Short conversation

  1. YouUne table pour deux, s’il vous plaît.A table for two, please.
  2. Other speakerOui.Yes.
  3. YouMerci.Thank you.
5

Asking directions

Use the station question to ask your way; the French and Russian phrases specifically refer to a railway station. Practise right and left as possible answers.

  • Où est la gare ?Where is the station?
    Pronunciation oo eh lah gar
  • Est-ce loin ?Is it far?
    Pronunciation es lwan
  • À droite.To the right.
    Pronunciation ah drwat
  • À gauche.To the left.
    Pronunciation ah gohsh

Short conversation

  1. YouOù est la gare ?Where is the station?
  2. Other speakerÀ droite.To the right.
  3. YouMerci.Thank you.
6

Hotels and asking for help

At reception, mention your reservation and ask about check-out and Wi-Fi. Show the final phrase if you need help.

  • J’ai une réservation.I have a reservation.
    Pronunciation zhay ün ray-zehr-vah-syon
  • À quelle heure faut-il libérer la chambre ?What time is check-out?
    Pronunciation ah kel uhr fo-teel lee-bay-ray lah shombr
  • Quel est le mot de passe du Wi-Fi ?What is the Wi-Fi password?
    Pronunciation kel eh luh mo duh pass dü wee-fee
  • Pouvez-vous m’aider ?Can you help me?
    Pronunciation poo-vay voo meh-day

Short conversation

  1. YouPouvez-vous m’aider ?Can you help me?
  2. Other speakerOui.Yes.
  3. YouQuel est le mot de passe du Wi-Fi ?What is the Wi-Fi password?

How to practise

Choose one situation each day, read the phrases aloud and swap roles. Latin pronunciation guides are approximate and cannot represent every sound or stress precisely; show the original text when needed.
