About Karshvar

I built Karshvar to make discovering the world easier for people who love reading history. My biggest difficulty was always geography: from Anatolia to the Caucasus, from the Iranian plateau to Byzantium, I kept losing track of where important events happened.

So I started studying geography, and this game grew out of that learning. It is both a way to test myself and a step toward the offline companion I wanted for exploring the world. I plan to add more historical features over time.

Karshvar takes its name from an ancient Iranian word related to today’s Persian word for country. In Avestan cosmology, the world was described as seven regions.

How to speak Russian: everyday conversations for English speakers

Use these simple Russian phrases to greet people, shop, order food, ask directions and check into a hotel. Every phrase includes its English meaning.

1

Greetings and politeness

Start with a greeting and add please to requests. Use excuse me to get someone's attention politely.

  • Здравствуйте.Hello.
    Pronunciation ZDRAST-vuy-tye
  • До свидания.Goodbye.
    Pronunciation da svi-DA-ni-ya
  • Пожалуйста.Please.
    Pronunciation pa-ZHA-lu-sta
  • Спасибо.Thank you.
    Pronunciation spa-SEE-ba
  • Извините.Excuse me.
    Pronunciation iz-vi-NEE-tye
  • Да.Yes.
    Pronunciation da
  • Нет.No.
    Pronunciation nyet

2

When you do not understand

If you miss an answer, ask the speaker to slow down or repeat it before continuing.

  • Я не понимаю.I don't understand.
    Pronunciation ya nye pa-ni-MA-yu
  • Говорите медленно, пожалуйста.Please speak slowly.
    Pronunciation ga-va-REE-tye MYED-len-na pa-ZHA-lu-sta
  • Повторите, пожалуйста.Could you repeat that, please?
    Pronunciation paf-ta-REE-tye pa-ZHA-lu-sta

Short conversation

  1. YouГоворите медленно, пожалуйста.Please speak slowly.
  2. Other speakerДа.Yes.
  3. YouСпасибо.Thank you.
3

Shopping and payment

Point to the item, ask the price and check whether you can pay by card. Acceptance depends on the shop.

  • Сколько это стоит?How much is this?
    Pronunciation SKOL-ka EH-ta STO-it
  • Можно оплатить картой?Can I pay by card?
    Pronunciation MOZH-na ap-la-TEET KAR-tay
  • Мне это, пожалуйста.I would like this, please.
    Pronunciation mnye EH-ta pa-ZHA-lu-sta
  • Нет, спасибо.No, thank you.
    Pronunciation nyet spa-SEE-ba

Short conversation

  1. YouМожно оплатить картой?Can I pay by card?
  2. Other speakerДа.Yes.
  3. YouМне это, пожалуйста.I would like this, please.
4

Cafés and restaurants

Ask for a table, place your order and request the bill at the end. Without meat does not communicate every dietary restriction.

  • Столик на двоих, пожалуйста.A table for two, please.
    Pronunciation STO-lik na dva-EEKH pa-ZHA-lu-sta
  • Воды, пожалуйста.Water, please.
    Pronunciation va-DY pa-ZHA-lu-sta
  • Без мяса, пожалуйста.Without meat, please.
    Pronunciation byez MYA-sa pa-ZHA-lu-sta
  • Счёт, пожалуйста.The bill, please.
    Pronunciation shchot pa-ZHA-lu-sta

Short conversation

  1. YouСтолик на двоих, пожалуйста.A table for two, please.
  2. Other speakerДа.Yes.
  3. YouСпасибо.Thank you.
5

Asking directions

Use the station question to ask your way; the French and Russian phrases specifically refer to a railway station. Practise right and left as possible answers.

  • Где вокзал?Where is the station?
    Pronunciation gdye vag-ZAL
  • Это далеко?Is it far?
    Pronunciation EH-ta da-li-KO
  • Направо.To the right.
    Pronunciation na-PRA-va
  • Налево.To the left.
    Pronunciation na-LYE-va

Short conversation

  1. YouГде вокзал?Where is the station?
  2. Other speakerНаправо.To the right.
  3. YouСпасибо.Thank you.
6

Hotels and asking for help

At reception, mention your reservation and ask about check-out and Wi-Fi. Show the final phrase if you need help.

  • У меня есть бронь.I have a reservation.
    Pronunciation u mi-NYA yest bron’
  • Во сколько нужно освободить номер?What time is check-out?
    Pronunciation va SKOL-ka NUZH-na as-va-ba-DEET NO-mer
  • Какой пароль от Wi-Fi?What is the Wi-Fi password?
    Pronunciation ka-KOY pa-ROL at vay-FAY
  • Вы можете мне помочь?Can you help me?
    Pronunciation vy MO-zhe-tye mnye pa-MOCH

Short conversation

  1. YouВы можете мне помочь?Can you help me?
  2. Other speakerДа.Yes.
  3. YouКакой пароль от Wi-Fi?What is the Wi-Fi password?

How to practise

Choose one situation each day, read the phrases aloud and swap roles. Latin pronunciation guides are approximate and cannot represent every sound or stress precisely; show the original text when needed.
