About Karshvar

What is Karshvar?

I build and develop Karshvar in my spare time to make discovering the world easier and more enjoyable for people who, like me, love reading about human history and prehistory, and the natural history of Earth. My biggest difficulty was always keeping track of places on the map: from Anatolia to the Caucasus, from the Iranian plateau to the Americas, I would lose track of where important events happened and get confused by the different names used in each period.

So I had to learn geography, and this game/encyclopedia grew out of all that learning. It is both a way to test myself and a step toward a fun offline companion for exploring the world.

My ultimate aim is to build a visual tool for exploring the history of the world’s peoples, Earth’s natural history, and human prehistory. A tool that helps me see what the world was like, even before self-awareness existed on Earth! For me, discovering the past is more enjoyable than predicting the future.

Why the name Karshvar?

I am an Iranian from Khorasan, with a deep attachment to Iranian/Ir culture, which I feel part of. The name Karshvar comes from an ancient Iranian word related to the modern Persian word for “country.” In Avestan cosmology, the world had seven regions.
