◉ MISSION CONTROL ONLINE

MISSION: Protect Earth. Destroy incoming meteors before they hit. LOOK & AIM: Move your mouse to turn and aim. Hold the left mouse button to fire. MOVE: W / S moves forward / backward; A / D moves sideways. Space rises; Shift descends. RELOAD: Press R to replace your 30-round magazine. FLIGHT: Q / E rolls your body. Use opposite thrust to slow down or stop. VIEW: V switches camera. Hold Ctrl and move the mouse to look around the astronaut; you can still fire. Hold the right mouse button to zoom. CONTROLS: Esc pauses. M toggles sound. H opens help.
