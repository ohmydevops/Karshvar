Ambient game music

Original short ambient score authored for this project in save-earth-music.js.
This music is synthesized by Web Audio sine/triangle oscillators; it is not a
recording, a sampled commercial track, or an imitation of a named composer.
No third-party music assets or licenses are used.

A slow D-minor-centered four-chord cycle uses four-second chord crossfades,
sparse high notes, quiet filtered harmonics and a short feedback delay.
The score ducks under radio speech. Its timing uses AudioContext.currentTime
and the existing game audio tick, so pause suspends its musical timeline and
the sound/mute control applies to it with the rest of the game audio.
