Actual rocket launch recorded at New Smyrna Beach, Florida, about 20 km from
Kennedy Space Center; Schoeps CCM41/CCM8 and Sound Devices MixPre6, December 2021.
Adaptation for Karshvar: excerpt starting at 31 seconds, mono 22050 Hz PCM,
65 Hz highpass to remove inaudible sub-bass, normalized, with a 100 ms loop
crossfade (7.9 seconds), played at 1.25x. Runtime gain, lowpass
filter and stereo placement follow the rocket's distance and listener heading.
