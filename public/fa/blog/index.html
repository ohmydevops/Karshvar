دربارهٔ کَرشوَر

کَرشوَر رو ساختم تا کشف کردن دنیا برای کسایی که به خوندن تاریخ علاقه دارن راحت‌تر باشه. همیشه مهم‌ترین مشکل من این بود که وقتی تاریخ می‌خوندم، از آناتولی تا قفقاز، از فلات ایران تا بیزانس، جای همهٔ اتفاقات مهم رو توی جغرافیا گم می‌کردم.

مجبور شدم جغرافیا بخونم و این بازی نتیجهٔ همهٔ جغرافیا خوندن‌های منه؛ هم بهونه‌ای برای سنجیدن خودم، هم قدمی به سمت یه ابزار آفلاین کنار دستم که بتونم باهاش توی دنیا بچرخم. به‌مرور کلی ویژگی جالب تاریخی هم بهش اضافه می‌کنم.

اسم کَرشوَر از واژه‌ای کهن در زبان‌های ایرانی الهام گرفته که با «کشور» امروزی پیوند داره. در جهان‌شناسی اوستایی، جهان به هفت ناحیه تقسیم می‌شده.

ایران: پنج جای تاریخی برای کشف کردن

صفه‌ها و کاخ‌های هخامنشی در تخت جمشید شروع خوبی برای شناخت ایران باستان‌اند. کنار پلکان‌ها کمی مکث کنید؛ نقش هیئت‌ها و جزئیات لباس‌هایشان داستانی فراتر از عظمت کاخ روایت می‌کند.

Russia: Five historic places to discover

Between the Caspian Sea and the mountains, Derbent guarded a narrow passage. Its Sasanian fortifications explain that geography in stone. Follow the relationship between the citadel, the walls and the old town to understand why this location mattered.

Türkiye: Five historic places to discover

These Neolithic enclosures take the story far earlier than classical cities. The T-shaped pillars are the first things to notice, followed by their animal carvings. They offer a glimpse of communal and ritual life long before the familiar empires of Anatolia.

France: Five historic places to discover

This Roman aqueduct bridge is an engineering lesson you can read from the riverbank. Its stacked arches carried water across a difficult landscape. Look at the rhythm of the stonework and imagine the longer water supply system of which this was only one part.

English

Saudi Arabia: Five historic places to discover

Hegra’s Nabataean tombs turn sandstone outcrops into monumental architecture. Their carved façades are the main attraction, but inscriptions and wells broaden the story. Look for differences between façades rather than treating every tomb as the same design.

English

Iraq: Five historic places to discover

Babylon is inseparable from the history of Mesopotamian cities. Its archaeological remains invite you to imagine a major political and religious centre. Distinguish surviving ancient material from later reconstruction when reading the site: both affect what a visitor sees.

English

United States: Five historic places to discover

Mesa Verde in Colorado preserves Ancestral Pueblo places, including cliff dwellings and mesa-top settlements. Look beyond the dramatic alcoves to the communities that lived and farmed here. These are places of continuing significance to descendant Indigenous peoples.

English

Egypt: Five historic places to discover

The pyramids are the familiar starting point, but the wider burial landscape gives them context. Around ancient Memphis, royal tombs, temples and smaller burial structures reveal how much effort went into preparing for the afterlife. Look beyond the skyline and imagine the people who planned, built and maintained these places.

English

Italy: Five historic places to discover

Rome rewards looking at several periods together. The Forums and Pantheon recall the ancient city, while churches and public buildings show how later generations reshaped it. Choose a small part of the historic centre and follow those changes on foot: the interest lies as much in the connections between monuments as in each landmark.

English

Greece: Five historic places to discover

Above Athens, the Acropolis gathers several monuments from the city’s classical past. The Parthenon is the familiar landmark, but the entrance buildings and smaller temples help explain the hill as a whole. Consider how the structures relate to one another: this was a deliberately shaped sacred place, not a single temple standing alone.

English

India: Five historic places to discover

The Taj Mahal is a memorial before it is a famous photograph. Shah Jahan commissioned the white-marble mausoleum in Agra in memory of his wife. Keeping that purpose in mind gives its monumental scale a personal dimension. Pause over the relationship between the building’s overall form and the craftsmanship that makes it feel so carefully composed.
