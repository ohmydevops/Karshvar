The publisher describes these as equirectangular planetary maps derived from
NASA elevation and spacecraft imagery. Color/shading is tuned to spacecraft
photos, with enhanced saturation; unmapped regions may contain compatible
fictional fill. These are planetary visualization maps, not live photographs.
Venus uses its cloud-covered atmosphere rather than an exposed surface map.
